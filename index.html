Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how Mekm, a student planner application, handles information. For privacy enquiries, contact the Mekm developer at mekm.app@gmail.com.

1. Information used by Mekm

Mekm does not require an account. Information you choose to enter or select may include:

  • Your preferred name and profile customisations.
  • Classes, teacher names, room and class codes, timetables, tasks, assignments, deadlines, calendar entries, notes and locations you manually enter.
  • Profile photos, backgrounds, banners and banner videos you select.
  • Theme preferences, focus timer settings, focus session totals and reminder preferences.
  • Local record identifiers, timestamps and Plus preferences and locally cached subscription status.

Mekm uses this information to organise your studies, display your planner, personalise the app and maintain your preferences. Avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information.

2. Local storage and backups

The current app stores planner records in a local SQLite database, preferences in local application storage and selected media in its application files. Mekm does not operate a backend for planner data or provide its own cloud synchronisation service.

Depending on your operating system, account and device settings, eligible app data may be copied to system-managed Android or Apple backups, including cloud backups or device transfers. These services are governed by the platform provider's policies and settings; Mekm does not manage them.

3. Permissions

Mekm uses the device's photo selection facilities to let you choose images and videos. On iOS it may request access to photos for that purpose. Mekm does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera or precise device location in the current version. A location you type into a planner entry is not GPS tracking. Reminder settings are currently stored locally; notification scheduling is not yet active.

4. Analytics, advertising and sharing

Mekm does not integrate Firebase, advertising, marketing trackers or a dedicated crash-reporting SDK. RevenueCat processes subscription activity for purchase management and subscription analytics. It does not transmit planner entries or selected media to developer-operated servers or sell personal information. Your operating system or app store may independently process diagnostic or distribution information under its own policies.

5. Mekm Plus and subscriptions

This section applies to versions of Mekm with live Plus subscriptions enabled. Google Play Billing handles Android purchases and payments, while RevenueCat acts as our subscription management service provider. Mekm does not receive your full payment card details.

RevenueCat uses a randomly generated anonymous app user identifier, purchase history, product identifiers, purchase tokens, subscription status and renewal or expiry information, together with relevant store, locale and currency information. This information is used to validate purchases, unlock Plus features, restore purchases, manage subscription access and understand subscription performance. The identifier is not your name or email and does not require a Mekm account. Planner content and selected media are not sent to RevenueCat.

Subscription information is transmitted securely and retained as necessary to manage subscriptions, restore purchases, resolve support enquiries and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by contacting us at the email below; we may need your RevenueCat app user identifier or purchase reference to locate your record. Deleting local app data does not cancel your subscription. You can manage or cancel Android subscriptions through Google Play.

Learn more in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

6. Retention, deletion and security

Planner data and media are retained in application storage until you remove them or clear application data. You can use Settings → Erase All Data to remove planner records, saved media and personal preferences; some non-personal test or subscription flags may remain. You can also remove application data using device settings where supported. Existing system backups may remain until removed under your platform's backup controls.

Mekm uses operating-system application storage and its access controls. Please protect your device with a screen lock and keep the operating system updated. No storage method can guarantee absolute security.

7. Contacting us and your rights

If you email us, we receive the information you send, including your email address, to respond to your enquiry. We use it for support and privacy requests and retain it only as needed for those purposes and applicable obligations. Our support email is hosted by Google. You can ask about access, correction, deletion, restriction or other privacy rights that apply to you by contacting us. Since planner data is local, we generally cannot access it or delete it remotely. UK users may also contact the Information Commissioner's Office.

8. Student privacy

Mekm is a study organisation tool. The current app does not require account registration or send students' planner records to us. Users should avoid including unnecessary information about other people in their planner or selected media. Parents and guardians can contact us with privacy concerns.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when Mekm's information-handling practices change. The most recent revision date appears above.
